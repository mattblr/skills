# Ormitar: four dots become a character

Fóir's starting logo had four green dots. The first mascot direction treated those as small dots above a larger body. That missed the idea: each dot was supposed to be a whole character.

The next version put four characters in a line. At rest, though, they needed to look like one. The useful change was to conceal three bodies behind the front character, then let them peek out. They could spread into a 2×2 formation when the state called for it. The round silhouette became the preferred demo shape.

The static logo took a separate path. More visible overlaps made the joins awkward, and lighter green variations weakened the mark. The final choice returned to the compact, clover-like silhouette and removed its eyes. The mascot kept its expressions.

This is a reconstructed design study based on that iteration, not a claim that avatar-me originally built Ormitar. The GIFs in the repository use Ormitar's authored geometry and sample its formation poses. They are illustrations of the process, not browser recordings.

The reusable lessons are about concealment, drawing order, and keeping a character recognisable at rest. A new project should supply its own geometry and colours. Four green blobs are specific to Fóir.

[Blobatar by Alain00](https://github.com/Alain00/blobatar) inspired the original exploration of playful generated avatars. Ormitar became an independent implementation, and avatar-me asks the agent to build a component owned by the project using it.
